Integrity monitoring

While the exam is running, this page records each time the exam window loses focus or is hidden (switching tab, window or app), with the time and the exact duration in seconds, plus page reloads and changes of the device clock. The log is attached to your submission and reviewed by the instructor; a focus event is not proof of misconduct. Right-click and copying are disabled for standard profiles. Accommodated profiles allow text selection and copying for approved accessibility tools, which may cause focus events. Reading mode and local audio are available to everyone; bring headphones.
